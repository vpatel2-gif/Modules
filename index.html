Cheese Omelette

Serves 1 · 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp (14 g) butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 30 g (1/4 cup) grated cheese
  • 1/4 small onion, sliced
  • 6 baby spinach leaves
  • A pinch of salt and pepper

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat until it foams, then soften the onion for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the baby spinach and cook for 1 minute, until wilted.
  3. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper, pour them into the pan, and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle the cheese over the eggs and fold the omelette in half. Cover for 1 minute.
  5. Slide the omelette onto a plate and serve hot.

Margherita Pizza

Serves 1 · 1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g (3/4 cup + 1 tbsp) All-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp instant yeast
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 60 ml (1/4 cup) warm water
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 50 g (about 1/2 cup) grated mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast and salt in a bowl. Add the warm water and olive oil, then stir into a rough dough.
  2. Knead on a floured surface for 8 minutes, until smooth and springy.
  3. Put the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it, and leave it in a warm place for 1 hour, until it doubles in size.
  4. Pre Heat the oven to 240°C (465°F).
  5. Roll or stretch the dough into a round about 20 cm (8 in) wide.
  6. Spread the tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a 1 cm border. Top with the mozzarella.
  7. Slide the pizza onto the baking tray and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.
  8. Scatter the basil on top and serve.

Fettuccine Alfredo

Serves 1 · 20 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g dried fettuccine
  • 1 tbsp (14 g) butter
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 80 ml (1/3 cup) heavy cream
  • 30 g (1/3 cup) grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp pasta water
  • A pinch of salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Boil a pot of salted water and cook the fettuccine as the packet says (about 10 minutes).
  2. Before draining, scoop out 2 tbsp of the pasta water and set it aside.
  3. While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a pan over low heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds without browning it.
  4. Pour in the cream and simmer gently for 2 minutes.
  5. Turn the heat off and stir in the Parmesan until the sauce is smooth.
  6. Add the drained pasta and the pasta water. Toss for 1 minute until the sauce coats every strand.
  7. Season with pepper and serve straight away.

Adjust the seasoning to taste.
